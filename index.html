SM ATTARI – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026
Contact: smattari84@gmail.com

SM ATTARI ("we", "us", or "our") operates the SM ATTARI mobile shopping application. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you browse products, place an order, or track an order.

1. Information we collect

When you place an order, the app asks you to provide:

  • Full name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Delivery address
  • City
  • State
  • PIN code
  • Products ordered, quantities, prices, order number, order status, and order date

We use this information to process, deliver, support, and track your order.

You can browse the product catalogue without creating an account. The current app does not require account registration or sign-in to place an order.

2. How we use information

We use order and delivery information to:

  • Accept and process orders
  • Arrange delivery and contact you about an order
  • Provide order tracking when you enter the order number and matching mobile number
  • Maintain business, security, and transaction records
  • Respond to customer support requests and comply with applicable law

We do not use the information collected through checkout to serve behavioural advertising.

3. Where information is stored and service providers

Order details are transmitted to our backend service and stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted using Render and Neon infrastructure. These providers process or store information on our behalf to operate the app and its services. Their own terms and privacy policies may also apply.

We do not sell personal information. We do not disclose it to unrelated third parties for their own marketing. Information may be shared with service providers who help operate the service, delivery partners when needed to fulfil an order, or authorities when legally required.

4. Payments

The current app checkout flow records the order and delivery details. It does not request card, bank-account, or UPI credentials in the app. If payment methods change, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.

5. Data retention and deletion requests

We retain order information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil orders, handle customer service, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of record and applicable law.

To ask a question about your personal information or request deletion where legally possible, email smattari84@gmail.com with your order number and request. Do not send passwords, OTPs, or payment credentials. We may need to retain certain transaction records where required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect order information. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Children

SM ATTARI is a general shopping app and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Your choices

You may choose not to place an order if you do not want to provide the delivery details required for fulfilment. You may contact us at the email below with privacy questions or requests.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, our practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will show a revised effective date.
